# Google sign-in icon

`google-g.png` is the unmodified Google G asset from Google's official identity branding page:

- Asset: https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png
- Usage guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines

The sign-in button displays it at 20 × 20 pixels with its original aspect ratio and colors on a white background. The frontend offers Google sign-in/linking only.
